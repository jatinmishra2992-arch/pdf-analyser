Smart PDF Analyzer

System

  • AI modelGoogle Gemini
  • BackendPython, Flask
  • PDF enginePyMuPDF
  • HostingRender (free)

How it works

  1. Your PDF is split into parts of about 10 pages.
  2. Gemini writes notes for each part, in the original order.
  3. The parts are joined into one set of notes.

Good to know

  • Limit: 100 pages and 32 MB.
  • The first visit can take a minute, because the free server sleeps.
  • Your PDF is sent to Google's AI. Please avoid private documents.
  • Diagrams are marked in the notes but not copied in yet.
